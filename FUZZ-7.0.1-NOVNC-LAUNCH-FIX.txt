Fuzz 7.0.1 - noVNC launch fix

Problem:
The 7.0 Cloud page embedded the separate vnc.* Cloudflare/noVNC origin in
an iframe. In some browsers that can remain on the Fuzz "Opening noVNC"
screen indefinitely even though noVNC works when opened directly.

Fix:
- Connect to PC navigates directly to the working noVNC launch URL.
- No iframe.
- No Scramjet/Ultraviolet layer.
- No fake desktop loading workspace.
- "Open in new tab" remains available.
- Browser Back restores the Connect button normally.
- /api/cloud/config reports embedded=false for compatibility with older UI.

Changed:
- index.js
- package.json
- package-lock.json
- static/cloud.html
- static/assets/js/cloud.js
